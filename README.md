# GreenHavenMC

Official legal documentation for **GreenHavenMC**, a Discord bot that verifies Minecraft accounts and manages access to our Minecraft server.

## About GreenHavenMC

GreenHavenMC connects Discord accounts with Minecraft accounts to ensure that only verified members of our Discord community can join our Minecraft server.
