# GreenHavenMC — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

GreenHavenMC is a Discord bot designed to verify Minecraft accounts and manage access to our Minecraft server.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information collected through our verification system.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## 2. Information We Collect

GreenHavenMC may collect and store the following information:

- **Discord User ID:** Used to identify your Discord account.
- **Minecraft Username:** Used to link your Minecraft account to Discord.
- **Minecraft UUID:** Used to identify your Minecraft account even if your username changes.
- **Discord Server Membership:** Used to confirm that you are a member of our Discord community.
- **Verification Status:** Used to determine whether your Minecraft account is authorized to join the server.
- **Verification Logs:** Records of account linking, verification attempts, and administrative actions when necessary.

GreenHavenMC does not request your Discord password or Minecraft account password.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used exclusively for:

- Linking Discord accounts to Minecraft accounts.
- Verifying membership in our Discord server.
- Managing the Minecraft server whitelist.
- Preventing unauthorized server access.
- Enforcing our community rules.
- Maintaining the security and functionality of our verification system.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or third parties.

## 4. Account Linking

Each Discord account is permitted to link one Minecraft account unless an administrator authorizes otherwise.

Only verified Discord community members are eligible to access our Minecraft server.

If you leave or are removed from our Discord server, your Minecraft server access may be revoked.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Verification information may be stored in a database or server configuration files accessible to authorized administrators.

We use reasonable security measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Sharing

GreenHavenMC does not sell or share verification information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be accessed by authorized server administrators when necessary to manage accounts, enforce community rules, or resolve technical issues.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain verification information for as long as necessary to operate the account verification and server access system.

If you leave our Discord community or unlink your Minecraft account, your server access may be removed.

You may request deletion of your stored verification information by contacting a GreenHavenMC server administrator.

Certain records may be retained temporarily when necessary for security, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.

## 8. Third-Party Services

GreenHavenMC operates using Discord and Minecraft-related services.

Your use of these platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

- [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy)
- [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)

## 9. Children's Privacy

GreenHavenMC is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements in their country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, verification system, or legal obligations.

The latest version will be published in our official GitHub repository.

## 11. Contact and Support

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request access to or deletion of your verification information, please contact the administrators of our official GreenHavenMC Discord server.
